Sunburn Arena 2026

Goa, India  ·  Stage & Concert Lighting

Project Brief

Full-Scale Stage Lighting For Asia's Biggest EDM Festival

BSM was commissioned to design and deploy a complete lighting rig for the main stage at Sunburn Arena 2026, one of Asia's largest electronic dance music festivals. The brief called for a system capable of running flawlessly across three consecutive nights of high-intensity performances in front of over 100,000 attendees.

The final rig combined 120 BSM Beam 380 moving heads, 80 Wash 1940 fixtures, a custom laser array and a fully redundant DMX control backbone — delivered on schedule and within budget.
