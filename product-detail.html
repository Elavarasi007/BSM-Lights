Overview

The BSM BEAM 380 is engineered for professional lighting designers and rental companies who demand power, precision and reliability. With its advanced optical system and dynamic effects, it is perfect for concerts, live events, clubs and large-scale productions.

Powerful Output

380W high-brightness discharge lamp.

Precision Optics

2.0° sharp beam for long throw.

Dynamic Effects

18 Prism, Frost & Gobo Wheel.

Smooth Movement

540° Pan & 270° Tilt.

Technical Specifications

Model BSM Beam 380
Power Supply AC 100–240V, 50/60Hz
Power Consumption 550W
Light Source 380W Discharge Lamp
Beam Angle 2.0°
Color 14 Colors + White
Prism / Gobo 18 Prism + Frost / 17 Gobos
Pan / Tilt 540° / 270°
Control Mode DMX512, Auto, Sound, Master/Slave
Weight 18.5 kg
