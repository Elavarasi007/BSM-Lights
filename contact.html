Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for dealers?

Minimum order quantities vary by product range — our sales team will share exact MOQs based on your dealer tier and region during onboarding.

Do you offer on-site installation and support?

Yes, our technical team provides on-site installation, programming and after-sales support for large venue and festival projects across India and select international markets.

What warranty do BSM products carry?

All BSM fixtures come with a standard 2-year manufacturer warranty covering manufacturing defects, with extended warranty plans available for rental fleets.

Can I request a custom lighting design for my event?

Absolutely. Share your venue details and requirements through the contact form above and our design team will prepare a tailored lighting proposal.
