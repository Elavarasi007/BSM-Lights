WHO WE ARE

Leaders In Professional Lighting Solutions

BSM Lighting Innovation is one of India's leading manufacturers and suppliers of professional stage, entertainment, architectural and commercial lighting systems. We deliver innovative lighting technologies designed for concerts, theatres, television studios, clubs, hotels, wedding venues and large-scale events.

Our mission is to empower lighting professionals with premium-quality fixtures, intelligent control systems, dependable technical support and continuous innovation. Today, BSM products are trusted by dealers, rental partners and lighting designers across the globe.
